How Orb Tangle handles on-device game data, advertising and privacy choices.

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy describes the Orb Tangle iOS app (“Orb Tangle” or “the app”). The app is published by Cato Martinussen, an individual, using CM Simple Works as its developer brand. Contact details are at the end of this policy.

Information stored on your device

The app stores gameplay information locally so it can keep your progress. This can include completed levels, stars, best move counts, unlocked levels, a current unfinished attempt, hint balances, rewarded-ad event records, and sound or vibration preferences. These values are stored using iOS local preferences and are not synced to an Orb Tangle account.

Local game records remain on the device until they are removed by the app, the app’s data is reset, or the app is uninstalled. The app does not currently provide account registration or cloud synchronization.

Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

Orb Tangle uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request banner ads and optional rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are optional; bonus hints are added only after the SDK confirms that a reward was earned. The app does not operate its own account or advertising server.

When ad services are used, Google’s SDK and its service providers may process information such as IP address, device and advertising identifiers, app and device details, ad impressions and interactions, diagnostics, performance data, and approximate location inferred from IP address. This information may be used to provide, measure, secure and improve advertising services. The data and purposes depend on the SDK version, device settings, ad request, regional consent choices and Google’s services. See Google’s iOS data disclosure and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Consent and tracking choices

The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to refresh consent information and present consent or privacy-choice forms where required. It requests ads only when UMP indicates that ads may be requested. If a privacy-options form is available for your region, you can reopen it in the app’s Settings.

After ads may be requested, the app can ask for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission on iOS if its status is undetermined. You can change this permission in iOS Settings. If you deny tracking permission, ads may still be requested without access to IDFA; consent and regional settings also affect ad requests. The app’s current ad request does not set a child-directed or under-age-of-consent treatment flag.

Children and audience

Orb Tangle is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. The app does not ask players to provide personal information or create an account. This description is not an App Store age rating; the rating and age-related App Store disclosures are determined separately during App Store Connect submission.

Website and other providers

The Orb Tangle website is a static GitHub Pages site and does not include its own analytics or advertising scripts. Hosting providers may process technical request data under their own terms. External links, including Google services and GitHub support, are covered by those providers’ privacy practices. The app’s Google SDK processing is described above.

Your choices and questions

Game progress, settings and hint balances are stored on your device and are not synced to an Orb Tangle account. You can manage iOS tracking permission in Settings and in-app consent choices in Orb Tangle Settings when available. Removing the app removes its locally stored game data. The app does not currently offer an account-based export or in-app data deletion tool.

For privacy questions or requests, contact masterbindertcg@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with a new update date.
