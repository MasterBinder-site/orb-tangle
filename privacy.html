This policy describes the Orb Tangle iOS app (“Orb Tangle” or “the app”). The developer responsible for the app is [Developer or legal entity name to be confirmed]. This policy should be updated if the app, its SDKs or its data practices change.

Information stored on your device

The app stores gameplay information locally so it can keep your progress. This can include completed levels, stars, best move counts, unlocked levels, a current unfinished attempt, hint balances, rewarded-ad event records, and sound or vibration preferences. These values are stored using iOS local preferences and are not synced to an Orb Tangle account.

Local game records remain on the device until they are removed by the app, the app’s data is reset, or the app is uninstalled. The app does not currently provide account registration or cloud synchronization.

Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

Orb Tangle uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request banner ads and optional rewarded ads. A rewarded ad is shown only when you choose to watch it. The app grants bonus hints only after the ad SDK confirms that the reward was earned.

Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app and device details, ad impressions and interactions, diagnostics, performance data, and approximate location inferred from IP address. The information and purposes can depend on the SDK version, your device settings, the ad request, applicable consent choices and Google’s services. Google describes the SDK’s data practices in its iOS data disclosure documentation.

Google and its service providers may use this information to deliver, measure, secure and improve advertising services, subject to the choices and controls available in your region and on your device. See Google’s Privacy Policy for information about Google’s processing.

Consent and tracking choices

The app integrates Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent information and show consent or privacy-choice forms where applicable. Ads are requested only when the SDK indicates that ads may be requested. You can reopen privacy choices from the app’s Settings when that option is presented. UMP privacy messages and regional requirements may change.

On iOS, the app may request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) authorization when the system status is undetermined and ad requests are allowed. You can change tracking permission later in iOS Settings. If you decline ATT, Google documents that ads may still be requested without the IDFA. Your ATT choice does not remove local game progress.

Other providers and websites

This website is a static GitHub Pages site and does not intentionally use analytics or advertising scripts. Opening an external link, including a GitHub support request or Google policy page, is subject to that provider’s own privacy terms. The Orb Tangle app itself relies on Google’s advertising and consent SDKs as described above.
