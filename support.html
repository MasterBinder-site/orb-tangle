01 · Controls

How to play

  1. 01

    Swipe in any direction

    All orbs move together in a single move.

  2. 02

    Read the stops

    Walls, board edges and other orbs stop movement. An orb can be a useful blocker for another.

  3. 03

    Match every color

    Guide each orb to its matching goal. The level is complete when every orb is aligned.

02 · Progress

Levels

Orb Tangle has 100 levels, with new strategies introduced as you progress. Complete levels to open more puzzles. Level 51 also requires 135 total stars.

03 · Scoring

Stars

Each level awards up to five stars. Solving in fewer moves relative to that level’s PAR earns a higher rating. Your best result is kept.

★

Used a helpful hint?
That attempt is capped at three stars. A fresh attempt without a hint can earn five.

04 · Hint bank

Hints

  • Five free daily hints renew at local midnight.
  • Unused daily hints do not carry over.
  • Daily hints are spent before bonus hints.
  • Bonus hints stay in your bank between days.
  • A completed rewarded ad grants three bonus hints.
  • You can watch up to five rewarded ads per day. Watching is optional.
  • A hint only counts when it provides a suggested move.

05 · Answers

Frequently asked

Why did one orb stop early?

Another orb, a wall or the edge of the board stopped it. That can be part of the solution.

Can I keep daily hints for tomorrow?

No. The daily allowance resets to five at local midnight. Bonus hints are saved across days.

Does watching an ad guarantee hints?

Three bonus hints are added only after the rewarded ad reports that its reward was earned. Closing an ad early does not award hints.

Can I replay a completed level?

Yes. Your best star rating and move count are preserved when you improve or replay a level.

Where can I contact support?

Use the Orb Tangle support request page on GitHub. GitHub issues are public, so do not include private information. A direct support email will be added when confirmed.

Still stuck?

Send us a note.

Open a support request on GitHub. Please include your device model and level number, but leave out personal or account information.
